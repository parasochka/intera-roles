1. Scope

This policy covers intera-roles.com and the communication that follows from it: request forms, email exchanges and Early Adopter onboarding. It does not cover data your own company processes inside its systems of record.

2. What we collect

Only what you send us and what a web server records by default:

  • Name, work email, company, role and industry, when you fill in a request.
  • The description of the operational problem you choose to share.
  • Technical request data: IP address, browser, pages visited, timestamps.

We do not ask for, and do not want, personal data of your customers or employees in a first contact.

3. How we use it

To answer your request, to prepare the first role or integration discussion, and to keep a record of the agreement we reach with you. We do not sell contact data and do not run advertising profiling.

4. Data inside INTERA

INTERA connects to your systems with read-only access and writes nothing back. In a local installation, the operational data stays in your infrastructure and we never receive it. When we assist with onboarding, any access we are granted is scoped, time-limited and documented with your IT team.

5. Sharing

We share the minimum necessary with the service providers that host this site and deliver our email. Where an implementation partner is involved in your project, we tell you before anything is shared with them.

6. Retention

Request data is kept while the conversation is active and for 24 months afterwards, so we can pick the thread back up. Server logs are kept for 90 days.

7. Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Write to the address in section 10 and we will confirm within 30 days.

8. Cookies

This site uses functional cookies needed to serve pages and remember your consent choice. Analytics cookies, if enabled, are described in the cookie policy and are set only after you agree.

9. Changes

The version and date at the top of this page change whenever the text does. Material changes are noted in the release information.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy go to sb@by-sky.net.
