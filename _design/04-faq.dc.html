INTERA Roles

What is an INTERA Role?

A ready-made module built around a real responsibility — finance control, operations oversight, revenue assurance, customer health, system integrity. It arrives with its metrics, its checks and its detection logic, reading from several systems at once.

Who is it for?

Managers who work across several systems, cannot fully trust the numbers they receive, and need visibility without chasing other teams. In practice: operations and COO, finance and billing, revenue assurance, customer operations, commercial, and the department heads above them.

How long until we see something useful?

Days, not quarters. Once a data source is connected, the first reconciliation usually finds something. Value grows as more history accumulates and patterns become visible.

Can we change a Role?

Yes. Adjust metrics, change dashboards, add your own data sources, extend the logic. Roles are a starting configuration, not a fixed product.

How is this different from a dashboard?

A dashboard shows numbers and waits for someone to interpret them. INTERA watches the numbers, compares what should agree, raises the items that need action, and shows what keeps happening around them.

Is INTERA a BI tool?

No. BI tools help you analyse data and build reports. INTERA is for monitoring an operation across systems: role-based views, reusable business logic, and signals that need attention. If your problem is charts, use a BI tool. If your problem is that important signals are scattered across ERP, CRM, billing, spreadsheets and internal tools, that is what INTERA is for.

Do we need technical skills to use it?

No. Once the sources are connected, everything is presented for business users — no SQL, no query building. Connecting a new source is the one task that involves IT.

Is it free?

There is a free plan for evaluation: 3 roles, 10 users, 3 integrations, 30 days of history. Early Adopters get 12 months free with unlimited roles. See pricing.

INTERA Method

What is the Method?

An on-site engagement where we work with your team to find the blind spots, define the metrics that reflect reality, connect the sources and build the first working roles.

How is it different from consulting?

Consulting ends with recommendations. The Method ends with a running system: connected sources, defined logic, dashboards your team keeps using after we leave.

How long does it take, and who takes part?

Several intensive on-site days, depending on how many systems and roles are involved. We need operational leads, finance or billing stakeholders, and whoever owns access to the systems.

What do we prepare in advance?

Little. Access to the main systems, a few sample reports or exports, and the people who know how the work actually happens — not how it is documented.

What do we have at the end?

A working environment, connected sources, defined metrics and logic, dashboards in daily use — and a clear view of issues that were invisible before.

IT and data

Does INTERA write to our systems?

No. Access is read-only. ERP, CRM and billing remain the systems of record; INTERA reads from them and keeps its own logic on top.

What data can INTERA use?

ERP and accounting systems, CRM, billing and mediation, operational platforms, Excel exports, internal APIs and manual inputs. The value comes from combining them, not from any single one.

What exactly do we ask IT for?

A named system, a DataSource, the fields to map and a refresh window. The integration contract in the docs states it in one page.
